JARVIS privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

JARVIS is a personal assistant that Elliot Mason runs on his own computer for his own Google account. It has a single user, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Google data it accesses

  • Google Calendar: to read the owner's events, and to create or change events when the owner confirms.
  • Gmail: to read and summarise the owner's messages, apply labels, and create drafts. Messages are sent only after the owner explicitly confirms. JARVIS cannot delete email.

How it is used and stored

  • Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests.
  • Sign-in tokens and any notes JARVIS keeps are stored on the owner's own computer and personal cloud storage. They are not shared, sold, or used for advertising.
  • When the owner asks it to, JARVIS may pass the text needed for a request (for example, an email he wants a reply drafted to) to an AI model provider he has chosen, solely to produce that answer.
  • JARVIS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing JARVIS there stops all access immediately.
