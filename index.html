JARVIS

A private, single-user personal assistant.

JARVIS is a voice and text assistant that Elliot Mason runs on his own computer for his own use. It is not a public service, and no one else can sign up for it.

What it does

  • Reads his Google Calendar to answer questions such as "what's on tomorrow?", and adds or changes events only when he confirms.
  • Reads his Gmail to summarise his inbox, and drafts replies. Emails are sent only when he explicitly confirms.
  • Keeps timers, reminders and notes on his own computer.
